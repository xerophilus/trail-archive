Trail Archive

Your whole running history, on your device, searchable.

One payment, no subscription.

Trail Archive imports your Strava bulk export and individual Garmin/watch files into a searchable archive stored entirely on your device. No account, no server, no uploads. Everything stays local.

Getting your Strava export:

  1. Go to Strava Account Download
  2. Click "Request Archive" (don't worry, you're not deleting your account)
  3. Wait for the email with your download link (usually within an hour)
  4. Download the ZIP file and drag it here

All processing happens in your browser. No data leaves your device. Map tiles are the only network requests.
